Build a mobile application called "TrueTie".

TrueTie is a relationship commitment platform where couples earn rewards by maintaining loyalty and long-term commitment.

The app should include:
- User registration
- Couple linking system
- Loyalty tracking system
- Reward unlocking system
- Milestone tracking
- Trust scoring model
- Admin dashboard
- Secure authentication

Create a couple linking feature where:

- User A can send a relationship invite link to User B
- User B must accept the invite
- Once accepted, both profiles are permanently linked
- Store couple_id in database
- Only 1 active partner allowed at a time
- If breakup occurs, reset loyalty score

- Tracks relationship duration (days together)

- Tracks milestone dates (1 month, 3 months, 6 months, 1 year)
- Calculates a loyalty score based on:
    - Daily check-ins
    - No break status
    - Consistent activity
- Deduct points if inactivity exceeds 7 days

TrueTie is a relationship commitment platform designed to encourage loyalty, trust, and long-term connection between couples.
The app links two partners and tracks their commitment through daily mutual check-ins and milestone achievements.
Couples earn a dynamic Trust Score based on consistency, engagement, and relationship duration.
As their loyalty grows, they unlock exclusive rewards, digital badges, and special benefits.
TrueTie combines behavioral tracking with gamification to make commitment measurable and meaningful.
The platform also ensures privacy and secure authentication for both partners.
With a structured reward model and trust-based system, TrueTie transforms real love into a growth-driven journey.
